Since the September issue convene.md has gone from **1,201 conferences to 1,950**, and from 710 upcoming to **1,376 across 72 countries**.

None of that came from crawling more aggregators. It came from going through all 46 specialties one at a time and asking which societies were missing. Not obscure meetings: flagship congresses whose organisers simply weren't on the list. The AAO-HNSF. The North American Spine Society. The European Association of Urology. The American College of Radiology. The volume, it turns out, sits in subspecialty societies and in course programmes that repeat in a different city every few weeks. CIRSE's ESIR calendar alone accounted for eighteen entries across nine countries.

Nine countries are new: [Bahrain](https://convene.md/country/bahrain/), [Bangladesh](https://convene.md/country/bangladesh/), the [Czech Republic](https://convene.md/country/czech-republic/), [Egypt](https://convene.md/country/egypt/), [Kyrgyzstan](https://convene.md/country/kyrgyzstan/), [Panama](https://convene.md/country/panama/), [Romania](https://convene.md/country/romania/), [Saint Lucia](https://convene.md/country/saint-lucia/) and [Serbia](https://convene.md/country/serbia/).

## Six worth a look

**Soufrière, Saint Lucia: DAN's 100th Diving and Hyperbaric Medicine CME, 3–10 April 2027.**
Divers Alert Network has been running these courses since 1982 and the hundredth is a full week under the Pitons, provided jointly with the Undersea and Hyperbaric Medical Society and designed primarily for physicians. A new country on the map, with exactly one entry on it. [Course page](https://dan.org/event/dan-100th-diving-hyperbaric-medicine/) · [Saint Lucia](https://convene.md/country/saint-lucia/)

**Japan, March to June 2027: twenty-three congresses, the first of them in blossom season.**
The Japanese societies publish years ahead and cluster their annual meetings in spring, which makes the whole quarter plannable now. Paediatric nephrology opens in Kyoto on 9 March (IPNA's 21st congress, with the developmental nephrology workshop running on from the 13th); the 12th World Glaucoma Congress returns to Kyoto 20–23 April; internal medicine is in Osaka 9–11 April; rehabilitation medicine goes north to Hakodate 10–13 June. [Everything in Japan](https://convene.md/country/japan/) · [Kyoto](https://convene.md/city/kyoto/)

**Bangkok: 11th ASEAN Rehabilitation Medicine Association Congress, 2–5 December 2026.**
A month out, at the Royal Orchid Sheraton on the river, held jointly with the Royal College of Physiatrists of Thailand and the Thai Rehabilitation Medicine Association. [Congress site](https://arma2026.com/) · [Thailand](https://convene.md/country/thailand/)

**Bali: ISPRM 2027 World Congress, 12–17 June 2027.**
The world congress of physical and rehabilitation medicine, hosted by the Indonesian society. Worth marking now rather than later: abstracts close on 31 December. [Congress site](https://isprm2027.com/) · [Indonesia](https://convene.md/country/indonesia/)

**Athens: 28th EFORT Annual Congress, 19–21 May 2027.**
Europe's largest cross-border orthopaedics and traumatology meeting, at the Megaron. Early-bird registration runs to 11 February. [Congress site](https://congress.efort.org/) · [Greece](https://convene.md/country/greece/)

**The United Kingdom: 74 new entries, the largest jump of any country.**
Nearly all of it royal colleges and specialty societies: single-day courses in Belfast, Bristol, Southampton and Birmingham that no aggregator carries, because nobody is selling tickets to them. [Everything in the UK](https://convene.md/country/uk/)

Everything else, 46 specialties by country, city or year, is at [convene.md/browse](https://convene.md/browse/).

## How the dates get checked

Every entry links to the organising society's own page, never an aggregator, and the ones on machine-readable providers are re-checked weekly. Two things that caught this month, both worth telling you about.

An interventional radiology meeting in Washington was listed two days early: Thursday to Saturday, when it actually runs this coming Saturday and Sunday. That one is now right, with four days to spare.

And the Bali congress above was on the map twice, once as Bali and once as Nusa Dua, which is a district of Bali, because the society's own events page and the congress's own site disagreed about where it was. The congress site wins. One of the two entries is gone.

If you spot one that's wrong, reply to this email. It goes to a person.
