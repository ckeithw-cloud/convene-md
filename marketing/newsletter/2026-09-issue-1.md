Hello —

This is the first monthly note from **convene.md**, the map of physician conferences with dates and venues verified on the organising society's own site. You signed up on the site sometime in the last month; this is the one email a month it promised.

Since mid-August the map has grown by **174 upcoming conferences** to **1,201 across 76 countries**. Most of the additions are national and regional society meetings that don't show up on the big aggregators. A few stand out as trips.

## Six worth a look

**Vilamoura, Portugal — Congresso Português de Cardiologia, 18–21 March 2027.**
The Portuguese Cardiac Society's annual congress, at the Centro de Congressos do Algarve. March on the Algarve is mild and quiet. [Congress site](https://spc.pt/evento/congresso-portugues-de-cardiologia-cpc2027/) · [everything in Portugal](https://convene.md/country/portugal/)

**Reykjavik, Iceland — six conferences, a new country on the map.**
The 41st Scandinavian Congress of Rheumatology at Harpa (25–28 Aug 2027), the Nordic Stroke Society Congress (15–17 Sep 2027), a five-morning anesthesia update at the Iceland Parliament Hotel (23–27 Aug 2027, 20 AMA PRA Category 1 credits), and two CME cruises that sail from Reykjavik in July and August 2027. Late August is the window — after the crowds, before the dark. [Everything in Iceland](https://convene.md/country/iceland/)

**Athens, Greece — Hellenic Society of Cardiology International Congress, 13–15 November 2026.**
At the Grand Hyatt Athens, English-language programme. Close enough to plan now. [Congress site](https://www.hcs.gr/event/diethnes-kardiologiko-synedrio-2/) · [everything in Greece](https://convene.md/country/greece/)

**The Nordic rotation — Copenhagen, Tampere, Turku, Stockholm.**
The Nordic federations rotate their congresses between countries and publish years ahead: urology in Copenhagen (9–12 Jun 2027), ENT in Turku (26–28 May 2027), obstetrics & gynaecology in Tampere (24–27 Aug 2027), nephrology at the Karolinska in Stockholm (29 Sep–1 Oct 2027). Finland is new on the map with four. [Denmark](https://convene.md/country/denmark/) · [Finland](https://convene.md/country/finland/)

**Kahuku, Oahu — 25th Annual Hawaii Cardiovascular Symposium, 21–24 October 2026.**
Cedars-Sinai's long-running symposium on Oahu's North Shore. One month out. [Course page](https://cedars.cloud-cme.com/course/courseoverview?P=0&EID=33730)

**Kyoto, Japan — Internal Medicine for Primary Care, 10–13 November 2026.**
A Medical Education Resources destination course at the Four Seasons Hotel Kyoto, in autumn-colour season. [Course page](https://www.mer.org/conference/MTMwMw==/)

## A few by specialty

**Cardiology** — 21 added. Beyond Vilamoura and Athens: the German Cardiac Society's annual meeting in Mannheim (31 Mar–3 Apr 2027), the French Society's JESFC in Paris (13–15 Jan 2027), and the Iceland cardiology & critical-care cruise (7–14 Aug 2027). [All cardiology](https://convene.md/specialty/cardiology/)

**HPB / transplant surgery** — AHPBA 2027 in Miami Beach (11–14 Mar 2027), the American Transplant Congress in Seattle (5–9 Jun 2027), and the IHPBA World Congress in Vancouver (5–8 Apr 2028). [All HPB / transplant](https://convene.md/specialty/hpb-transplant-surgery/)

**Physical medicine & rehabilitation** — AAPM&R's Annual Assembly is confirmed for Phoenix (10–13 Nov 2027) and Denver (11–14 Oct 2028). [All PM&R](https://convene.md/specialty/physical-medicine-and-rehabilitation/)

**Palliative & supportive care** — AAHPM's Annual Assembly in Pittsburgh (17–20 Mar 2027) and the EAPC World Congress in Glasgow (20–22 May 2027). [All palliative care](https://convene.md/specialty/palliative-and-supportive-care/)

Everything else — 46 specialties, by country, city or year — is at [convene.md/browse](https://convene.md/browse/).

## How the dates get checked

Every entry links to the organising society's own page, never an aggregator, and the ones on machine-readable providers are re-checked weekly. This month that caught its first real change: a San Diego oncology course had quietly moved from September to October. If you spot one that's wrong, reply to this email — it goes to a person.
