About convene.md

A map of physician conferences, built by a practising surgeon

convene.md was built to help physicians find conferences across the globe — the flagship annual congresses, and the national societies, chapter meetings and destination CME courses that rarely make it onto the big lists. Everything is on one map, filterable by specialty, so a week of credits in a city you actually want to visit is a few clicks away rather than an evening of searching.

Right now it holds more than 950 upcoming conferences in 68 countries, across 46 specialties.

How the dates are checked

Every entry links to the organising society's or accredited provider's own page — never a conference-listing aggregator. Dates and venues are taken from that page. The entries hosted by the large academic CME providers are re-checked automatically every week, so a meeting that moves shows up as a change to be verified rather than sitting wrong for months.

What is listed, and what is not

  • Listed: in-person meetings that offer physician CME — AMA PRA Category 1 Credit™ or the national equivalent abroad (EACCME, national college accreditation and so on).
  • Not listed: online-only activities; meetings accredited only for nurses or other professions; and events from commercial "international conference on…" operators, however plausible the venue.

The bar is deliberately conservative: better to miss a meeting than to list a wrong one.

Get in touch

Missing a conference, spotted a wrong date, or want to talk about something else? Email hello@convene.md — it reaches a person, and every message is read. Conferences can also be submitted straight from the map with the Add a conference button; each one is verified before it goes live.
