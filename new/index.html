New on the map — September 2026

174 verified conferences added since August. This page is updated monthly; the same round-up goes out by email.

Most of what was added this month is national and regional society meetings that don't show up on the big lists. A few stand out as trips.

Six worth a look

Vilamoura, Portugal — Congresso Português de Cardiologia, 18–21 March 2027

The Portuguese Cardiac Society's annual congress, at the Centro de Congressos do Algarve. March on the Algarve is mild and quiet. Congress site · everything in Portugal

Reykjavik, Iceland — six conferences, a new country on the map

The 41st Scandinavian Congress of Rheumatology at Harpa (25–28 Aug 2027), the Nordic Stroke Society Congress (15–17 Sep 2027), a five-morning anesthesia update at the Iceland Parliament Hotel (23–27 Aug 2027, 20 AMA PRA Category 1 credits), and two CME cruises that sail from Reykjavik in July and August 2027. Late August is the window — after the crowds, before the dark. Everything in Iceland

Athens, Greece — Hellenic Society of Cardiology International Congress, 13–15 November 2026

At the Grand Hyatt Athens, English-language programme. Close enough to plan now. Congress site · everything in Greece

The Nordic rotation — Copenhagen, Tampere, Turku, Stockholm

The Nordic federations rotate their congresses between countries and publish years ahead: urology in Copenhagen (9–12 Jun 2027), ENT in Turku (26–28 May 2027), obstetrics & gynaecology in Tampere (24–27 Aug 2027), nephrology at the Karolinska in Stockholm (29 Sep–1 Oct 2027). Finland is new on the map with four. Denmark · Finland · Sweden

Kahuku, Oahu — 25th Annual Hawaii Cardiovascular Symposium, 21–24 October 2026

Cedars-Sinai's long-running symposium on Oahu's North Shore. One month out. Course page

Kyoto, Japan — Internal Medicine for Primary Care, 10–13 November 2026

A Medical Education Resources destination course at the Four Seasons Hotel Kyoto, in autumn-colour season. Course page · everything in Kyoto
