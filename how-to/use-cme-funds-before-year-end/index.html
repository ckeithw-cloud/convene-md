CME funds expiring December 31? What you can still do

Last reviewed 13 October 2026 · Updated each autumn

Every employer's rules are different, and they are the only rules that matter here. Nothing below is a substitute for your own benefits policy or the person who administers it. The sections that follow are mostly about which questions to ask, because the answers vary between institutions in ways no article can predict.

Most hospital and group CME allowances run on the calendar year and do not roll over. If yours expires on 31 December, the money is genuinely gone on 1 January — and a surprising number of physicians discover this in the second week of December, when the realistic options have narrowed considerably.

The one question that decides everything

Ask your administrator: does my allowance pay out on the date I register and pay, or on the date the conference actually takes place?

This single answer determines what is still possible:

  • If it pays on the date of payment — you can register now for a conference anywhere in 2027 and claim it against this year's money. This is the version that rescues a December scramble, and it is common but not universal.
  • If it requires the activity to occur within the benefit year — you need a meeting that happens before 31 December, and your options are the ones still on the calendar.

Ask it in writing, and ask it before you book anything. Two other things worth confirming in the same message: whether the allowance covers registration only or travel and lodging as well, and what the reimbursement submission deadline is — it is often weeks before 31 December, which catches people out more often than the expiry itself.

If the activity has to happen this year

There is more left on the calendar than most people assume. As of today there are 401 conferences still to come before 31 December, 99 of them in December itself. Browse everything left in 2026, or filter the map by your specialty to see what is reachable.

Two practical notes on late booking. Registration for December meetings is usually still open in October and November, but hotel room blocks close earlier than registration does — the conference rate typically expires three to six weeks out, after which you are paying rack rate. And if the destination is a ski town or a resort, December is high season; the meeting may have space when the flights no longer do.

If prepaying next year's meeting counts

This is the better outcome, because it lets you choose properly rather than taking whatever is left. Register and pay in December for a meeting in 2027, keep the receipt dated this year, and submit it before your deadline.

Worth knowing: many societies open registration for their annual meeting in the autumn of the preceding year, and early-bird rates are usually the lowest price the meeting will ever be. So the deadline that feels like a constraint is often the cheapest moment to book. Browse 2027 and 2028 by specialty, country or city.

What to check before you commit

  • Credit type. If you need AMA PRA Category 1 Credit™ for licensure or board certification, confirm the activity actually designates it — not every meeting does, and some offer only contact hours. Every conference on this site links to the organising society's own page so you can check the accreditation statement directly.
  • Cancellation terms, if there is any chance your schedule moves.
  • Whether your institution requires pre-approval before you book, which some do and which cannot be obtained retroactively.

A note for next year

The physicians who never have this problem do one thing differently: they pick the meeting in January and register when early-bird opens, rather than looking in November. If that sounds obvious, it is — and the reason it does not happen is that finding the right meeting is tedious, which is the entire reason this site exists.
